## Phase 1: Ideation

- Five problem statements are announced at 10:00 AM. Pick **exactly one**.
- AI allowed: **ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, free versions only**, and **only ONE platform per team**.
- The number of prompts is restricted, so use them judiciously.
- Mandatory upload by 11:00 AM: website title, solution description, key features, and images, references or visual concepts (if any).

## Phase 2: Development

**AI may be used for:**
- clarifying programming concepts and syntax
- understanding code logic and implementation approaches
- debugging and resolving errors
- guidance on technical implementation
- improving workflow efficiency

**AI must NOT be used for:**
- generating complete websites or webpages
- generating source code or complete sections for direct use
- AI website builders or automated site generators
- any other form of directly AI-generated implementation

**Prompt limit:** maximum **5 prompts per team** for the whole development phase (ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, free versions). Faculty coordinators monitor this. Record each prompt in the *AI Prompt Log* of your `WORKFLOW.md`.

> ❗ **Direct AI generation of websites or their source code results in IMMEDIATE DISQUALIFICATION.**

## Tools & technologies

Use any stack you know: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, frameworks, libraries, editors and developer tools.

## General

- Plagiarism, unfair practices or breaking the guidelines may lead to immediate disqualification.
- Teams are responsible for making sure their submission is complete and accessible.
- Late or incomplete submissions may not be considered.
- **The judges' decisions are final.**
