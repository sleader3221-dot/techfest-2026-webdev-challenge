The problem

Your booking calendar decides which machines run together. So it also decides your load curve.

Small workshops take bookings without seeing what they do to the meter. When three customers pick the same evening slot, the shop can cross its sanctioned load, pay more for evening power, or turn a job away.

Over the limit

Oven, compressor and welder together reach 27 kW, which is 7 kW over a 20 kW connection.

Costlier hours

Evening power costs ₹10 per kWh against ₹7 in the day. The same work costs more at 7 PM.

A job turned away

If the welder is booked last, it doesn't fit. That's ₹1,800 of work lost.

Under the hood

How SlotWatt decides

No black box. Here is every step the simulator takes, and every formula behind the numbers.

Scheduling, step by step

  1. Place every fixed job at the time the customer asked for.
  2. For each flexible job, list every slot the rules allow.
  3. Drop slots that cross the load limit or run conflicting machines together.
  4. Pick the slot with the lowest load. If two tie, pick the earlier one.
  5. If no slot is left, flag the job for the owner with the reason.

Limits of this prototype

  • SlotWatt plans machine use. It does not switch equipment on or off.
  • It uses hourly average loads, so it does not model motor starting current.
  • It does not replace circuit protection or a qualified electrician.
  • All shop data and prices are sample values.
