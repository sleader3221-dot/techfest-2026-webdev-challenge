    How FairFix works

    Three steps to a fair deal, every time.

    1

    Neighbours report real prices

    After every job, residents log what they paid — including labour, materials and visit charge — in under 2 minutes.

    2

    You see the fair range

    FairFix shows the median price, the low-high range, and flags unusual reports — so you know exactly what to expect before calling anyone.

    3

    Post a request and bargain

    Post a job request with your offer. Providers see the neighbourhood median beside your bid, so negotiations start from a fair baseline.

    How trust works

    We built reliability into every number.

    Min. 3 reports

    We only show a median when we have at least 3 data points. Fewer than that shows "Not enough data yet."

    Outlier filtering

    Reports more than 50% from the median are flagged as "unusual" and excluded from the range calculation.

    Verified receipts

    Reports with a photo receipt upload earn a "Verified" badge, adding extra confidence.

    Circle-only visibility

    All prices are from RAMAIAH-BLK-A residents only. Data stays local to your community, not cross-city averages.

    Fair-price floor

    Offers below 70% of the median are blocked — protecting good providers while keeping prices fair for customers.

    Transparent counters

    Every counter-offer on the Job Board shows the neighbourhood median beside the customer's offer, so providers negotiate honestly.

    About FairFix

    Built to make local home services honest and predictable.

    Home repairs shouldn't feel like a guessing game. In most apartments and hostels, two neighbours on the same floor pay wildly different amounts for the exact same tap leak or AC service simply because nobody shares bills.

    FairFix turns your community's past invoices into a living, outlier-filtered price book — helping residents pay fair rates and helping honest local technicians build lasting trust.

    🔍 What's real vs simulated

    FairFix is a hackathon demo. Here's exactly what the engine does vs what's seeded.

    ✅ Real

    📊 Statistics engine (median, percentiles, outliers)
    💰 Overcharge checker with live ratios
    🤝 Bargain logic, floor, and fair-offer marker
    🔐 Role gating (customer vs provider)
    ⚡ Cross-tab sync via BroadcastChannel
    📒 Yearly spend ledger with charts
    💾 localStorage persistence with try/catch

    🧪 Simulated

    🏘 Neighbour circle & verification
    🌱 ~55 seeded sample reports
    🤖 "Simulate provider reply" in Demo Controls
    🔑 Demo logins (no real auth)
    📸 Receipt preview (no cloud upload)
